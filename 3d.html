How it plays

Touch first, with full keyboard and controller support.

  • Find the decade's key

    Every maze hides one. Take it before the clock does.

  • Reach the locked portal

    One key, one portal, one way out. Ten portals to the next decade.

  • Ladle the chili

    Serving chili keeps the burgers away. Two shots per maze, so make them count.

  • Outrun the burgers

    They want chili too. Contact ends the run.
